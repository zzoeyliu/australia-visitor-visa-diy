仅填已经确认的内容。方括号用于私下补全。说明信按需要使用，篇幅取决于需要解释的事实。

## 简短英文说明信

> Dear Visa Officer,  
> I plan to visit Australia from [arrival date] to [departure date] for [actual purpose]. My planned activities include [brief details]. [If visiting someone, state your relationship and where you plan to stay.]  
> [Explain who will pay for which costs and refer to the evidence available.]  
> I plan to leave Australia on [date] and return to [actual commitment, if relevant]. [Name the supporting document, if available.]  
> Thank you for considering my application.  
> [Applicant name and date, to be completed privately]

## 英文资助说明

> I am [relationship to the applicant]. I will cover [specific costs or confirmed amount] for [applicant] during the planned visit from [date] to [date]. I have provided [available supporting evidence] to show how I will cover these expenses.  
> [Supporter name, date and signature if required, to be completed by the supporter]

保留付款主体和范围。全额资助与只付住宿的陈述要区别；日常支出由谁负担尚未确认时，先问清。

## 英文缺件说明

> I am unable to provide [requested document] because [factual reason]. [If available, identify other evidence and what it shows.]

若本人确认从无该字段所问范围内的境外旅行经历，可简写为 `I have not travelled overseas before and therefore have no passport stamps for previous overseas travel to provide.` 如果曾免签旅行或旅行时未盖章，按实际经历修改。放在当前页面允许的如实说明入口。
